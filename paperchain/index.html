What it is

Remember the paper chain on the classroom wall in December? One link for every day until winter break, and every morning somebody got to tear one off.

Paper Chain is that, on your iPhone. Pull the bottom link down. It stretches, it strains, and then it gives. The scrap flutters to the floor and lands on the pile, and you're one link closer.

Count down to a date (Halloween, the trip, the wedding, the last day of school) and each link waits for its day. Or just count: thirty workouts, twelve chapters, ten piano lessons. When the last link goes, the whole pile goes up in the air. Then you sweep up and start the next one.

The wall

Real paper. Real physics. Your wall.

Every link is a loop of construction paper that swings when you tilt your phone, catches the light, and tears where it's weakest. Long countdowns wind onto a wooden spool that clicks round as the next link comes off.

Printed paper for the seasons, and rooms to match: a pumpkin patch at dusk.

Some rooms are outside. The wind picks up. Two chains to a room, and rooms are free, as many as you like.
