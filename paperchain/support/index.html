Support

Answers to the usual questions. If yours isn't here, write to us; a real person reads every email.

How do I tear a link?

Pull the bottom link down until it gives, right on the wall. A link that belongs to a later day waits for its day; pull it early and the chain tells you when it's ready.

I tore one by mistake.

Right after a tear, tap Tape it back over the scraps. The link goes back on the chain, with a little tape to show for it. The offer lasts a few seconds.

How do I share a chain?

Tap the chain's name under it, then Share. Paper Chain makes a short video of your chain tearing a link, with its name and count, to send or save.

How do I add a widget?
  • Home Screen: press and hold an empty spot, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Paper Chain.
  • Lock Screen: press and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose the Lock Screen, and tap the widget area.

To pick which chain a widget shows, press and hold the widget and tap Edit Widget.

How do reminders work?

A chain counting down to a date reminds you once, on the day a link is ready, and never nags about links you haven't torn. To change the time or turn them off, tap the chain's name, then Edit. Not getting them? Check Settings → Notifications → Paper Chain.

How do I change or delete a chain?

Tap the chain's name under it, then Edit. You can rename it, change its length or date, its paper and color pattern, or delete it.

What are rooms?

Each room holds two chains. Swipe sideways on the wall to move between rooms; the page after your last room starts a new one. Tap a room's name at the top to rename it or change how it looks. Rooms are free, as many as you like.

To swap the two chains in a room, press and hold one, slide it along the shelf, and let go.

What's the Party Pack?

The one thing for sale in Paper Chain: a single purchase that unlocks every paper and every room's look, including every new one we add later. It's shared with your family through Family Sharing. Everything else in the app is free.

I bought the Party Pack, but the papers are locked.

Tap any locked paper, then Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. For refunds or billing questions, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, since Apple handles every purchase.

Will my chains come to a new iPhone?

Your chains live only on your phone; there's no account or sync. If you set up your new iPhone from a backup of the old one, they come along. Deleting the app deletes them.

What does Paper Chain do with my information?

Nothing leaves your phone except a few anonymous counts, like how many chains get made. No names, no dates, nothing you type. The privacy policy lists every one.
